🚀 Quick Start Guide

  1. Click "Open Operator Panel" above
  2. Enter your name and click Login
  3. Click "New Game" button
  4. Select Ruleset: "RDCL" (or "RDCL half game")
  5. Add team names and configure settings
  6. Click "Start Game" and you're rolling!

✨ RDCL Features

  • 4 periods of 15 minutes each
  • 1-minute jams (not 2 minutes like WFTDA)
  • Continuous jam numbering across periods
  • RDCL penalty codes (X, B, L, MB, UC, etc.)
  • Per-half timeout rules automatically enforced
  • Half game variant available (2×15 min)
